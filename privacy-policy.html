Talkalong Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Talkalong doesn't collect your data. Your scripts, settings, videos and voice stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who is responsible

Talkalong is made by Nadiia Kindrachuk, an independent developer, who is responsible for this policy.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Scripts and settings are stored in the app on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Recordings are kept in the app only until your next recording. A video leaves the app only when you save or share it yourself, and it then goes where you choose, such as Photos, Files or another app. That place's own privacy policy applies from there.

Camera and microphone

Talkalong uses the front camera and the microphone only while you are on the teleprompter screen, to show the picture and to record the videos you start. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Speech recognition

When the app follows your voice, speech is recognized on your iPhone by Apple's on-device speech models. Talkalong doesn't send your audio or the recognized words anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Talkalong only learns whether a purchase is active. It never sees your name, email address or payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Talkalong collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and at this address, with a new effective date.

Contact
