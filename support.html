Talkalong Support

Talkalong is a teleprompter for iPhone: each word of your script lights up when it's time to say it, and the text scrolls by itself while the front camera records you.

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to kindrachuchka@gmail.com. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

What is free?

Everything in the app: writing scripts, the teleprompter and all its settings, recording and watching your takes, as often as you like. Your first 3 saved or shared videos are free too. A plan lets you save and share every video after that.

Which plans are there?

  • Yearly: renews every year until you cancel.
  • Weekly: renews every week until you cancel. New subscribers start with a 3-day free trial.
  • Lifetime: one payment, no renewals.

The app shows the current price for your country before you buy.

How do I save a video?

After you stop recording, the "Your take" screen opens. Tap Save or Share Video, then Save Video to put it in Photos, or pick another app. The take stays in Talkalong only until your next recording.

How do I restore a purchase?

On the Scripts screen, tap the ••• button, then Get Unlimited Saving, then Restore Purchases at the bottom. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Talkalong and tap Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the subscription renews, or before a free trial ends, so you aren't charged again. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I ask for a refund?

Payments go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my scripts and videos kept?

Only on your iPhone. Talkalong has no account and no server, so deleting the app deletes your scripts. Videos you've saved to Photos stay there.
